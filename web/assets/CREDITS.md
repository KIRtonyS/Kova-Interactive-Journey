## Room tiles and furniture (`assets/lpc/tiles/`)

Sheets from the LPC starter kit, https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/RPG_Maker_MZ_LPC_Starter_Kit, copied unchanged. They are collections by several artists:
Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum), Matthew Nash, Tuomo Untinen, Janna, Casper Nilsson, Xenodora and others.
`tiles/ATTRIBUTION.txt` is the kit's own list of who made what; it notes a few pieces whose author the kit could not name.
Licences: CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 (some pieces also CC-BY 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0 or CC0).

- `tiles/interior.png`: the game uses wallpapered walls, bookshelf, desk, kitchen counter, small table.
- `tiles/furniture.png`: the game uses window, painting, plants, lamp, sofa, chair.
- `tiles/inside.png`: the game uses wood floor, door.
- `tiles/bathroom.png`: the game uses tiled wall, mirror and counter, sink, toilet (by Matthew Nash).
- `tiles/toilet-new.png`: the toilet from `tiles/bathroom.png`, whitened by `test/build-art.js`.
